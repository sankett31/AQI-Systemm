Search for a city, then choose a nearby station to see its latest reported pollutant measurements.

The site searches for monitors within 25 km. Station data is not available for every city.

About the AQI estimate: AirScope converts available PM2.5 or PM10 station readings to the U.S. AQI scale. Official AQI uses time-averaged measurements, so this latest-reading estimate can differ from Google or government dashboards.
Station measurements: OpenAQ. City search: Open-Meteo Geocoding (GeoNames). Values and timestamps are supplied by upstream station providers; reporting delays and station coverage vary.
